# Chapter 1 review checklist

Human checklist for Verses `1.1`–`1.47`.

**Do not check items automatically.** Mark `[x]` only after a human completes that step.

Legend: each Verse block uses the same eleven gates.

---

## Gates (per Verse)

1. identity confirmed
2. source reference confirmed
3. Devanagari reviewed
4. transliteration reviewed
5. punctuation reviewed
6. Unicode normalization checked
7. source license/status checked
8. source checksum recorded
9. discrepancy documented
10. second-pass review complete
11. approved for canonical corpus
