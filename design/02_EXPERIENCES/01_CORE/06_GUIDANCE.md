# GUIDANCE EXPERIENCE

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved for Design Exploration
**Owner:** Product & Design
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# Vision

Guidance is where life's questions meet timeless wisdom.

It helps readers connect the situations they are living through with teachings from the Bhagavad Gita, creating a bridge between everyday experience and enduring wisdom.

Guidance does not replace judgment.

It creates a thoughtful path back to scripture.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Guidance is to help readers discover teachings that are relevant to the challenges, questions, and experiences they are facing today.

Guidance begins with life.

It always leads back to wisdom.

---

# Experience Promise

Guidance promises:

> **"I will help you discover wisdom that speaks to the moment you're living."**

It does not promise answers.

It promises perspective.

---

# Primary User Question

> **What wisdom can help me right now?**

Every interaction within Guidance should help answer this question.

---

# Why This Exists

People rarely approach wisdom in identical ways.

Some begin with a chapter.

Others begin with a verse.

Many begin with life itself.

A difficult conversation.

A loss.

A decision.

A moment of uncertainty.

Guidance exists so that these moments become opportunities to engage with the teachings rather than reasons to search elsewhere for immediate answers.

---

# Experience Philosophy

Guidance begins with the reader's lived experience.

It listens first.

It clarifies when needed.

It then introduces teachings that may help illuminate the situation.

Guidance should never replace personal judgment or become the final authority.

Its purpose is to gently guide readers back to scripture, where understanding and reflection can begin.

---

The intended journey through Guidance is:

Share

↓

Clarify

↓

Discover

↓

Read

↓

Guidance succeeds when readers naturally move into Verse, Journal, or both.

---

# Experience Goals

Guidance should:

* help readers feel understood,
* connect life situations with scripture,
* encourage personal reflection,
* preserve the authority of the Bhagavad Gita,
* and create confidence without creating dependence.

---

# Emotional Arc

The intended emotional progression is:

Uncertainty

↓

Being Heard

↓

Perspective

↓

Understanding

↓

Reflection

↓

Direction

The experience should replace isolation with thoughtful exploration—not certainty.

---

# Sacred Principles

Wisdom before answers.

Scripture before AI.

Questions before conclusions.

Reflection before advice.

Humility before certainty.

Every future Guidance feature should protect these principles.

---

# Experience Responsibilities

Guidance is responsible for:

* understanding the reader's question,
* identifying relevant teachings,
* providing thoughtful context,
* encouraging deeper reading,
* and inviting reflection.

---

# Non-Responsibilities

Guidance is not responsible for:

* making decisions,
* giving professional advice,
* replacing therapy,
* diagnosing emotional states,
* predicting outcomes,
* or becoming an endless AI conversation.

Its purpose is to guide readers toward wisdom, not replace their judgment.

---

# Relationship to Other Experiences

Guidance begins with life.

It then connects readers to:

Verse

↓

Journal

↓

Journey

Guidance is an entry point into the broader Antar experience rather than a destination of its own.

---

# Information Architecture

The Guidance experience prioritizes information in this order:

1. Reader's Situation
2. Clarifying Question (when needed)
3. Relevant Teachings
4. Verse
5. Reflection Invitation
6. Journal Invitation

Conversation exists to support reading—not replace it.

---

# Guidance Philosophy

Guidance should never pretend to have complete understanding.

When appropriate, it should ask thoughtful follow-up questions before suggesting teachings.

Readers should feel listened to rather than analyzed.

The experience should remain conversational without becoming conversational for its own sake.

---

# Scripture Relationship

Every Guidance experience should naturally arrive at scripture.

Relevant verses should remain the centerpiece.

AI explanations should support the reader's understanding after scripture has been presented.

The Bhagavad Gita remains the primary voice.

---

# AI Philosophy

AI exists to:

* understand context,
* identify themes,
* surface relevant teachings,
* ask thoughtful questions,
* and encourage reflection.

AI should never:

* claim certainty,
* tell readers what they should do,
* replace scripture,
* become emotionally authoritative,
* or discourage personal interpretation.

---

# Privacy Philosophy

Readers should feel completely safe sharing personal questions.

Guidance should communicate:

* respect,
* humility,
* confidentiality,
* and emotional safety.

Trust should remain more important than personalization.

---

# Interaction Principles

Interactions should feel:

* compassionate,
* thoughtful,
* calm,
* and purposeful.

The experience should avoid long conversational loops.

Every interaction should gently move readers toward wisdom and reflection.

---

# Motion

Motion should communicate gentle progression.

Transitions should reinforce the feeling of moving from uncertainty toward understanding.

Animations should remain subtle and respectful.

---

# Accessibility

Guidance should remain fully usable with:

* Dynamic Type,
* VoiceOver,
* TalkBack,
* reduced motion,
* keyboard navigation,
* multilingual support,
* offline fallbacks where appropriate,
* and high contrast.

The emotional quality of the experience should remain consistent regardless of accessibility preferences.

---

# Engineering Responsibilities

Guidance should:

* separate AI reasoning from scripture,
* support multiple AI providers,
* preserve reader privacy,
* gracefully handle unavailable AI services,
* maintain conversation context within the current session,
* and always provide a path to scripture.

Readers should never lose access to the teachings because AI is unavailable.

---

# Analytics Philosophy

Analytics should improve the quality of guidance rather than optimize conversation length.

Appropriate measurements include:

* successful transitions to Verse,
* Journal continuation,
* accessibility feature usage,
* reader satisfaction with suggested teachings.

Avoid measuring:

* conversation duration,
* dependency on AI,
* prompt volume,
* or engagement loops.

Success is measured by helping readers reconnect with wisdom—not by extending conversations.

---

# Success Criteria

The Guidance experience succeeds if:

* readers feel understood,
* relevant teachings are discovered,
* scripture remains central,
* AI feels humble,
* readers naturally continue into Verse and Journal,
* and Guidance encourages independent reflection.

---

# Failure Modes

The Guidance experience fails if:

* AI becomes more important than scripture,
* conversations never end,
* advice replaces wisdom,
* certainty replaces humility,
* readers stop engaging directly with the Bhagavad Gita,
* or the experience creates dependence on AI.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

* AI-first experiences,
* authoritative life advice,
* therapy simulation,
* diagnosis,
* endless chat,
* replacing scripture with summaries,
* engagement-driven conversations,
* emotional manipulation,
* or certainty without humility.

Guidance exists to illuminate—not to decide.

---

# Future Opportunities

Future versions may explore:

* multi-turn guided reflection,
* life-theme exploration,
* seasonal guidance journeys,
* richer verse relationships,
* multilingual guidance,
* and deeper integration with Journal and Journey.

Every future addition should strengthen the connection between life and scripture.

---

# Design Decisions

### Guidance always leads to scripture.

**Reason**

The Bhagavad Gita is the foundation of Antar. AI exists to help readers reach it—not replace it.

---

### Questions precede conclusions.

**Reason**

Understanding a reader's context produces more thoughtful guidance than making immediate assumptions.

---

### AI remains intentionally humble.

**Reason**

Wisdom invites reflection rather than certainty.

---

### Reflection follows guidance.

**Reason**

Personal meaning emerges after engaging with the teaching, not before.

---

# Open Questions

The following require validation through design and user testing:

* How often should Guidance ask clarifying questions before surfacing verses?
* How many relevant teachings should be presented at once?
* When should readers be encouraged to open Journal?
* What is the right balance between AI conversation and direct reading?
* How should Guidance behave when AI is unavailable?

---

# North Star

When readers leave Guidance, they should feel less alone in their questions and more connected to the wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita.

Guidance succeeds not by providing answers, but by helping readers discover teachings that illuminate their own path.
