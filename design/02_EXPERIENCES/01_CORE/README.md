# CORE EXPERIENCES

Core Experiences define the primary relationship between the reader and Antar.

They represent the essential journey through the product:

```text
Home
↓
Library
↓
Verse
↓
Journal
↓
Journey
```

Guidance provides an additional entry point that begins with a life situation and leads the reader back to Verse.

## Core Experience Files

* `01_VERSE.md` — Engage deeply with one teaching.
* `02_HOME.md` — Begin with clarity.
* `03_LIBRARY.md` — Discover without feeling overwhelmed.
* `04_JOURNAL.md` — Reflect without feeling judged.
* `05_JOURNEY.md` — Recognize growth without turning it into measurement.
* `06_GUIDANCE.md` — Connect present-life questions with relevant wisdom.

## Core Rule

Every Core Experience must:

* answer one primary human question,
* make one clear experience promise,
* preserve the authority of the Bhagavad Gita,
* reduce unnecessary cognitive load,
* remain accessible,
* and avoid engagement patterns that conflict with Antar's principles.

Core Experiences define product behavior and emotional intent.

They do not define implementation-specific component APIs.
