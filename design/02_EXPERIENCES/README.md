# EXPERIENCE DESIGN

This directory defines the major experiences people have within Antar.

Experiences describe:

- the human need being addressed,
- the promise made to the reader,
- the intended emotional journey,
- the information hierarchy,
- the interaction philosophy,
- success criteria,
- failure modes,
- and the boundaries of the experience.

Experiences are not reusable UI components.

They are composed from foundations, components, content, and system behavior.

## Relationship to Other Design Layers

* `01_FOUNDATIONS/` defines how Antar should feel across the entire product.
* `02_EXPERIENCES/` defines what a reader should experience.
* `03_COMPONENTS/` defines reusable interface building blocks.
* `04_PROTOTYPES/` validates design hypotheses before implementation.

Every experience should remain consistent with the Product Bible, Product Principles, Experience Bible, Information Architecture, and relevant system specifications.
