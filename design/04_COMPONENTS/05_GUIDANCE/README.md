# GUIDANCE COMPONENTS

Guidance components connect present-life questions with relevant teachings from the Bhagavad Gita.

Planned components include:

- Guidance Input
- Clarifying Question
- Teaching Recommendation
- Guidance Response

Guidance components should:

- lead readers toward scripture,
- communicate uncertainty honestly,
- avoid authoritative life advice,
- minimize unnecessary conversation,
- and remain usable when AI support is unavailable.

The final V1 model for theme-based versus free-text Guidance remains a deferred product decision.
