# ADR-005 — AI Is Never the Primary Experience

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Many AI products place conversation before understanding.

Antar intentionally reverses this order.

## Decision

Readers should:

Read

↓

Reflect

↓

Study

↓

Optionally speak with Saar

AI exists to deepen understanding rather than replace reading.

## Consequences

- Scripture remains primary.
- Reflection remains central.
- AI becomes a companion.
