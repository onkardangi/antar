# ADR-006 — Guidance Organizes Around Reader Intent

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Initial Guidance designs organized information by content categories.

Readers do not naturally think in taxonomies.

## Decision

Guidance begins with the question:

"Where would you like to go from here?"

Readers choose intentions such as:

- Understand
- Connect
- Learn
- Ask Saar

## Consequences

- Feels conversational.
- Supports multiple learning styles.
- Organizes around curiosity rather than content.
