# ADR-007 — Curated Understanding Precedes Conversation

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Conversation should not replace structured learning.

## Decision

The learning journey becomes:

Read

↓

Reflect

↓

Guidance

↓

Understanding

↓

Ask Saar

Curated knowledge is always presented before conversational AI.

## Consequences

- Readers encounter thoughtful explanations first.
- AI fills gaps rather than replacing study.
- Antar feels like a study companion instead of a chatbot.
