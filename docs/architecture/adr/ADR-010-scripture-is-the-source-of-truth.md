# ADR-010 — Scripture Is the Source of Truth

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Antar presents multiple layers of understanding:

- Bhagavad Gita
- Traditional Commentaries
- Curated Understanding
- Saar

Without a clear hierarchy, readers may not know what is scripture, what is commentary, and what is AI synthesis.

## Decision

Antar presents knowledge in the following order:

Scripture

↓

Traditional Commentary

↓

Curated Understanding

↓

Saar Conversation

Every experience should make this hierarchy explicit.

AI never speaks with the same authority as scripture.

## Consequences

- Preserves trust.
- Makes provenance clear.
- Keeps the Bhagavad Gita at the center of the experience.
- Prevents AI from becoming the perceived authority.
